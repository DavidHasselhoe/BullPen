What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Websites use them to make the site work, or to work more efficiently, and to remember information between visits.

Cookies set by the website owner (in this case, Hasselø BullPen) are called "first-party cookies." Cookies set by anyone else are called "third-party cookies."

Why do we use cookies?

We keep this deliberately narrow: BullPen does not use cookies for advertising, analytics, or interest-based tracking, and we do not permit third parties to use our Website for those purposes. We use cookies and similar local storage only where strictly necessary for the Website to function (such as security) or to remember a functional preference you've set (such as your selected language). Every cookie and storage item we actually use is listed in the table below — there are no undisclosed categories.

How can I control cookies?

When you first visit our Website, a cookie banner lets you choose to accept all cookies or continue with only strictly necessary cookies. Essential cookies cannot be rejected, as the Website cannot function securely without them. You can also set or amend your web browser's own controls to accept or refuse cookies generally, or clear your browser's stored data for this site to reset your earlier choice.
